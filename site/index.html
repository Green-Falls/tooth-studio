Your hygienist

Tooth Studio & Wellness is the practice of Rytel Rowe, an Independent Practice Dental Hygienist. Maine grants that license to hygienists with at least 2,000 hours of practice under a dentist's supervision. She has worked in dental care for more than ten years.

She does every cleaning herself, and you can text her to book or move a visit. About the studio

What a visit covers

All services

  • Cleanings

    Routine, gingivitis or periodontal, depending on the health of your gums.

  • Oral cancer screening

    A check of your lips, tongue, cheeks and throat.

  • X-rays

    Taken at the studio, to find decay between teeth and changes in the bone.

  • Fluoride

    Treatment that strengthens enamel, for kids and adults.

  • Sealants

    A protective coating for the chewing surfaces of back teeth.

  • Whitening

    In-office GLO Science whitening, usually done in one appointment. Whitening
