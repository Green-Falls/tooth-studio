Professional whitening

Since August 2025 Rytel has offered in-office whitening with the GLO Science Professional system. Each before-and-after result she has posted took one appointment.

How a whitening visit goes

  1. Rytel checks your teeth and gums. Whitening works best on clean, healthy teeth, so she may suggest a cleaning first.

  2. She holds a shade guide against your teeth to record your starting color.

  3. She protects your gums, applies the whitening gel and fits the GLO mouthpiece, which uses light and gentle heat. The gel goes on in short rounds.

  4. She checks your new shade against the guide with you before you leave.

Crowns and fillings

Whitening changes the color of natural enamel. Crowns, veneers and tooth-colored fillings stay the shade they are, so ask Rytel how they would match before you book.
