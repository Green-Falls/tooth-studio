Services

Tooth Studio is a hygiene practice, so every visit is preventive care: cleaning, x-rays, screening and protecting your teeth. Adults and children are both welcome.

  • One hygienist, every visit
  • Adults and children
  • Delta Dental and MaineCare

Cleanings

Your gums decide which cleaning you need, so they get checked first.

  • Routine cleaning

    Plaque and tartar come off your teeth and along the gumline, then a polish. The regular checkup cleaning for healthy gums.

  • Gingivitis cleaning

    For gums that are red, swollen or bleed when you brush. More of the visit goes into the gums, to bring the inflammation down.

  • Periodontal cleaning

    For gum disease that has gone below the gumline. A deeper clean, around the roots of the teeth.

Checks and protection

  • Oral cancer screening

    A look at your lips, tongue, cheeks and throat for sores or changes that need a closer look.

  • X-rays

    Radiographs show decay between teeth and bone loss that you can't see in the mirror. They're taken at the studio.

  • Fluoride

    Strengthens enamel. Common for kids, and useful for adults with sensitive teeth or a history of cavities.

  • Sealants

    A thin coating painted onto the chewing surfaces of back teeth. Kids get them most often, on new molars.

Children

Kids are seen as well as adults, and MaineCare covers both. Parents can book and move appointments by text.

Foster families use the studio too. One foster parent wrote that Rytel "makes it easy to get kids seen promptly and does an excellent job coordinating referrals to help them get caught up and stay on track with their dental health."

When you need a dentist

Fillings, crowns, extractions and other treatment come from a dentist. If your cleaning or x-rays turn up a problem, you'll hear what was found and get a referral to a dentist for the next step.

You can keep having your cleanings here while a dentist handles the treatment.

Visits outside the studio

An independent hygienist can bring her equipment and work on site. Rytel started with residents of Woodlands Memory Care of Bridgton in July 2024, four months before the Main Street studio opened. Residents get a cleaning and a check of their teeth and gums without leaving the building.

To book a visit for a family member at Woodlands, text or call (207) 599-6459, or send a visit request and choose "Visit at a care facility". If you run another care home, a workplace or a school in the Lakes Region, email the studio to ask about on-site visits.
