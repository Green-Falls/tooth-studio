# Tooth Studio & Wellness: research notes

Collected 2026-10-19 from the Facebook page (logged in, read only), the Google Business listing, Instagram (logged out), the federal NPI registry and the From the First Tooth referral list.

- **What it is:** an independent practice dental hygienist (IPDH) office in downtown Bridgton, Maine. It is a hygiene-only practice, not a dentist.
- **Legal name:** TOOTH STUDIO & WELLNESS LLC
- **Owner:** Rytel Rowe, IPDH.

## Services

These come from a Dec 2024 flyer and posts made between Jul 2025 and Oct 2026.

- Screenings, including oral cancer screenings
- Cleanings: routine, gingivitis and periodontal
- Fluoride treatments
- X-rays (radiographs)
- Sealants
- Professional teeth whitening:
  - Launched Aug 15, 2025, using the GLO Science system.
  - Posts claim "8 shades brighter in one visit" and "up to 14 shades brighter."
  - A $50-off back-to-school special ran in Sep 2025.
- Children and adults ("Welcoming children & adults").
- **Mobile / on-site care ("#wecometoyou"):**
  - Visits to Woodlands Memory Care of Bridgton started in July 2024. Booking dates were posted for Sept 20 and Oct 21, 2024.
  - A 2024 poll asked followers where they'd want a hygienist to come: workplace, care facility or school.
- Referrals to dentists for follow-up care. One review mentions that she coordinates referrals for foster children.
