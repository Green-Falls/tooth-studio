# Photo plan

The site has two photos of the current office (the entrance and the building sign). Everything else is from the first studio at 82 Main Street, Woodlands, or her whitening posts. Deep teal panels and icons fill the gaps until real photos arrive.

## Where to get photos, best first

1. **A short brand shoot with Ashleigh London Photography.**
   - She is a Lakes Region photographer (Naples and Bridgton) who photographed the studio's November 2024 open house and often supports the page.
   - Her Airbnb listing shows mini sessions from $125, so a one-to-two-hour shoot is a small cost. Confirm her current rates.
   - Ask her for the full-resolution open house photos too.
   - Sources: [her site](https://ashleighlondonphotography.mypixieset.com/about/), [Airbnb listing](https://www.airbnb.com/services/6978470).
2. **Rytel's phone.**
   - The Facebook copies are compressed. Ask for the original files of the 82 Main Street and 55 Main Street photos and the whitening results.
   - Ask for new phone photos of the current treatment room, using the shot list below. Daylight, lights on, no flash.
3. **Instagram and Facebook reels.**
   - The 40 Instagram posts and at least seven reels were never downloaded, because research ran logged out.
   - Stills from a reel of the current office could fill a slot.
4. **Her Google Business Profile.**
   - Photos uploaded there by her or by patients. Only usable with her permission.

## Stock and free-licence options, and why they're last

- **Free stock (Unsplash, Pexels).**
  - Free to use without attribution.
  - Search found nothing of Bridgton itself.
  - Generic dental stock (smiling patients, tools, chairs) is exactly what Bridgton Dental Associates and Main Street Dental use, and a lake sunset is Mountain View Dentistry's hero. Stock would pull the site back toward the competitors (see `qa/competitors.md`).
  - Stock models must never stand in for Rytel, her patients or her office.
  - If stock is used at all, keep it to non-identifying still life (a toothbrush cup, floss, a towel warmer) or a Lakes Region season texture.
- **Creative Commons.**
  - A Wikimedia photo of Pleasant Mountain from Denmark, Maine, which you can see from Bridgton. Licence is CC BY-SA 3.0, so it needs a credit line. That credit would go on a credits page, not as a caption.
  - Link: [Pleasant Mountain View](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?curid=20494550).
  - Nothing found of Bridgton itself.
- **Historical Bridgton.**
  - The Bridgton Historical Society's c. 1938 photos of Main Street buildings are on the Maine Memory Network, for example [17-19 Main Street](https://www.mainememory.net/record/79624).
  - Use beyond low-resolution educational copies needs the Society's permission.
  - A 1938 view of the block near 55 Main Street could suit the About page, if they agree.
- **AI-generated images.** Not for this site. Showing a made-up room or a made-up hygienist on a health practice's site misleads patients.

**Consent:** any patient who appears, even partly, needs written permission for web use.

**Practical:**
- Shoot in daylight and turn the room lights on.
- Keep a few frames with empty space on one side, for text.
- Send the original files, not screenshots or social media copies.
