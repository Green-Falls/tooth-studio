About the studio

Tooth Studio & Wellness is a one-hygienist practice on Main Street in Bridgton, owned by Rytel Rowe, IPDH.

How it started

Rytel worked as a dental hygienist for years before opening her own practice. Maine lets a hygienist with at least 2,000 hours of supervised practice apply to become an Independent Practice Dental Hygienist and see patients without a dentist on site. She registered Tooth Studio & Wellness in May 2024 and began with on-site cleanings at Woodlands Memory Care that July.

The first studio opened behind the red doors of 82 Main Street, Unit 1, with an open house on November 23 and 24, 2024: raffles, cocoa and tooth-shaped cookies. In April 2025 the practice moved down the street to the first floor of 55 Main Street, where it is today.

When Bridgton Dental Hygiene Care closed in October 2025 after its hygienist retired, its patients were invited to switch to Tooth Studio. The studio also collects for Bridgton's Free Little Pantry, runs giveaways, and has supported the Maine Dance Center recital and the Lake Region Cheerleading raffle.
