# AI slop audit: one-page site v1 (commit 5fbab6e)

Grade: **D+**. The facts were right and nothing was invented about prices or hours, but the design and copy came straight off the generic "wellness landing page" shelf. Swap the logo and it could be any spa, yoga studio or med-spa template.

## Design (3/10)

| Pattern | Where it showed up | Why it reads as AI |
|---|---|---|
| Uppercase tracked "eyebrow" label above every heading | 10 sections ("SERVICES", "PATIENTS SAY", "VISIT & BOOK") | The single most common tell of generated landing pages |
| Cream + sage + serif "spa" palette | Whole site | Default palette for anything tagged "calm" or "wellness"; nothing from her real brand (black script logo, the teal 55 Main sign) |
| Cormorant Garamond + DM Sans | Whole site | Stock "elegant" pairing |
| Arched "portal" photo crops | Hero and Visit, mirrored | Decorative shape with no source in the brand |
| Equal card grids with a little accent dash | 6 service cards, 3 insurance cards | Grid-of-cards is the reflex layout for any list |
| Zigzag image/text splits | Whitening, Mobile, About | Alternating left/right on autopilot |
| Pill buttons, primary + ghost pair | Every section | Template default |
| Pulsing dot "now welcoming" badge | Hero | Startup-launch trope |
| Trust strip of bullet facts under the CTA | Hero | Template default |
| Centered dark band explainer | IPDH section | Template default |
| ★★★★★ glyphs on every testimonial card | Reviews | Decoration, not information; Google already shows the rating |
| Sticky blurred glass header | Header | Template default |
| Same padding and shape for every section | Whole page | Metronome rhythm |

## Copy (stop-slop score 24/50)

| Dimension | Score | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Directness | 6 | Facts are there, wrapped in slogans |
| Rhythm | 4 | Every H2 is a short declarative fragment |
| Trust | 5 | Rhetorical questions, hand-holding |
| Authenticity | 4 | Reads like a template, not a Bridgton hygienist |
| Density | 5 | Filler sentences that say nothing |

Specific offenders:

- **Slogan headlines:** "Everything a good hygiene visit should be." "Simple to pay for." "Small, local and unhurried." "Find us on Main Street." "A brighter smile, usually in one visit." Each one is a pull-quote that tells you nothing.
- **"Not X, Y" contrast:** "care that feels calm, not clinical" as the H1. It's her phrase, but leading with a binary contrast is the formula.
- **Invented embellishment:** "appointments are booked with room to breathe", "most people see the difference before they leave", "so people can get care in the place that feels like home". None of these come from the research.
- **Rhetorical question:** "Wondering whether whitening is right for your teeth?"
- **Wh- opener:** "When getting to the studio is hard, we come to you."
- **Cutesy:** "where cavities like to start", "for kids and grown-ups alike".
- **Reflex triplets:** "cleaning, screening and prevention"; "Cleanings, screenings and whitening"; "food drives, giveaways, and support".
- **Passive voice:** "Appointments are by booking only", "Radiographs taken in the studio".
- **Thin social proof:** a "10/10!" Facebook comment used as a pull quote.
- **Boilerplate:** "This site does not give medical advice."
- **Undisclosed old photos:** the hero room is the first studio at 82 Main Street, shown as if it were the current office.

## What v2 does instead

- Uses her real brand: the thin ruled box from her logo as the layout motif, square corners, and the teal from the 55 Main Street sign as the one accent.
- Headings say what the page holds ("Insurance", "Booking an appointment"), with no labels stacked above them.
- Lists and tables for lists of facts. Cards only where there's a reason.
- Photos carry captions with the place and date, so the 82 Main Street photos are labelled as the first studio.
- Copy sticks to what the research shows, in plain sentences, with sources and dates on reviews.
- A real multi-page brochure site: Home, Services, Whitening, Care outside the studio, New patients, About, Contact.
