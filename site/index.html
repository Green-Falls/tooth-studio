What's an IPDH?

A hygienist with her own practice.

An Independent Practice Dental Hygienist is a licensed dental hygienist with the authority to practice on her own. Tooth Studio is a hygiene practice, not a dental office: your visit is about cleaning, screening and prevention, with time to ask questions. If you need care that a dentist provides, Rytel will refer you and help coordinate it.

Services

Everything a good hygiene visit should be.

  • Cleanings

    Routine cleanings, plus gingivitis and periodontal cleanings when your gums need more attention.

  • Screenings

    A careful look at your teeth and gums at every visit, including an oral cancer screening.

  • X-rays

    Radiographs taken in the studio, so you and your dentist have a clear picture.

  • Fluoride

    Fluoride treatments to strengthen enamel, for kids and grown-ups alike.

  • Sealants

    A thin protective coating for the chewing surfaces of back teeth, where cavities like to start.

  • Whitening

    Professional in-studio whitening with the GLO Science system. More below.

Professional whitening

A brighter smile, usually in one visit.

Tooth Studio offers in-studio whitening with GLO Science Professional. It's done by your hygienist, in the same calm room as your cleaning, and most people see the difference before they leave.

Wondering whether whitening is right for your teeth? Ask at your next cleaning, or send a text and we'll talk it through.

About the studio

Small, local and unhurried.

Tooth Studio & Wellness is owned by Rytel Rowe, IPDH, who has more than ten years of experience in the dental field. She opened the studio in downtown Bridgton in November 2024 and moved to 55 Main Street in April 2025.

Every visit is one-on-one. You'll see the same hygienist each time, you can reach her by text, and appointments are booked with room to breathe.

The studio is part of the town, too: food drives with Bridgton's Free Little Pantry, giveaways, and support for local teams and studios.
