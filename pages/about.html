About the studio

Rytel Rowe owns Tooth Studio & Wellness and does the cleanings herself. She has worked in dental care for more than ten years.

Rytel Rowe, IPDH

Rytel worked as a dental hygienist before she opened her own practice. Maine lets experienced hygienists apply to become Independent Practice Dental Hygienists and see patients without a dentist on site. She registered Tooth Studio & Wellness in May 2024 and began with on-site cleanings at Woodlands Memory Care that July.

The studio opened at 82 Main Street, Unit 1, with an open house on November 23 and 24, 2024: raffles, cocoa and tooth-shaped cookies. In April 2025 it moved down the street to the first floor of 55 Main Street.

When Bridgton Dental Hygiene Care closed in October 2025 after its hygienist retired, Rytel invited those patients to switch to her.

Around town

In May 2026 the studio collected food for Bridgton's Free Little Pantry, with a raffle entry for each donation. It has run Easter and May giveaways, advertised in the Maine Dance Center recital program and donated to the Lake Region Cheerleading raffle.
