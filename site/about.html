About the studio

Rytel Rowe owns Tooth Studio & Wellness and does every cleaning herself. She has worked in dental care for more than ten years.

Rytel Rowe, IPDH

Rytel worked as a dental hygienist in other offices before she opened her own practice. In Maine, a hygienist with enough clinical experience can apply to become an Independent Practice Dental Hygienist and see patients without a dentist on site. Rytel registered Tooth Studio & Wellness in May 2024 and started with on-site visits at Woodlands Memory Care that July.

She opened the studio on Main Street in November 2024 with an open house, and moved down the street to number 55 in April 2025. When Bridgton Dental Hygiene Care closed in October 2025 after its hygienist retired, Rytel invited those patients to switch to her.

Around town

The studio collected food for Bridgton's Free Little Pantry in May 2026, with a raffle entry for each donation. It has run Easter and May giveaways, advertised in the Maine Dance Center recital program, and donated to the Lake Region Cheerleading raffle.
