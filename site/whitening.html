Before, then after one visit

How a whitening visit goes

  1. Your teeth and gums get checked. Whitening works best on clean, healthy teeth, so you may be offered a cleaning first.

  2. A shade guide held against your teeth records your starting color.

  3. Your gums are protected, the whitening gel goes on, and the GLO mouthpiece activates it with light and gentle heat, in short rounds.

  4. Before you leave, the shade guide shows how far you've come.

Crowns and fillings

Whitening changes the color of natural enamel. Crowns, veneers and tooth-colored fillings keep their shade, so ask how they would match before you book.

Cost

Insurance plans treat whitening as cosmetic and usually don't cover it. Text the studio for the current price. Specials run now and then, like $50 off for back-to-school in September 2025, and appear first on Facebook and Instagram.
