What is an Independent Practice Dental Hygienist (IPDH)?

An IPDH is a licensed dental hygienist who can run their own practice in Maine without a dentist on site, after at least 2,000 hours of practice under a dentist's supervision. An IPDH cleans teeth, takes x-rays and gives preventive care, and refers patients to a dentist for treatment such as fillings.
