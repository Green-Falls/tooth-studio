Care outside the studio

An independent hygienist can pack up her equipment and work on site. Rytel started doing this in July 2024, four months before the Main Street studio opened.

Woodlands Memory Care

Rytel holds cleaning days for residents of Woodlands Memory Care of Bridgton. Residents get a cleaning and a check of their teeth and gums without leaving the building, which matters for people who find a trip to an office hard or upsetting.

Families book a resident's visit the same way as any appointment: text or call (207) 599-6459, or use the booking page and choose "Someone in a care facility".

Other places

If you run a care home, a workplace or a school in the Lakes Region and want hygiene visits on site, text or email Rytel to talk it through.
