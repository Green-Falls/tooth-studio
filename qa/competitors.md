## Where v4 was too close

The v4 hero ("Dental hygiene on Main Street in Bridgton." + "Request a visit" + "or call (207) 599-6459") followed the Bridgton Dental Associates formula almost line for line.

## What v5 does instead

- **Identity from her logo, which no one else has.** The hero is drawn as her logo is built: a thin ink frame whose top line the headline breaks, with "55 MAIN STREET · BRIDGTON, MAINE" in spaced capitals on the bottom line, like "DENTAL HYGIENE CARE" under her wordmark. No script font is added; hers stays the only one.
- **Lead with what a dental office can't say.** "Teeth cleaning in Bridgton, booked by text." The primary button is "Text (207) 599-6459", not "Request Appointment". The copy says she's a hygienist you text directly, the same person every visit, and taking new patients.
- **No shared visual vocabulary.** No serif display, no tan or navy, no pill buttons, no circular or arched photo crops, no labels over headings. Real photos of her building instead of stock dental photography.
- **Avoid their words.** "Relaxing", "gentle", "compassionate" and "modern" appear only inside quoted reviews and her own October 2026 post.
