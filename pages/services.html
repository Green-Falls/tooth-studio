Services

Tooth Studio is a hygiene practice. Rytel takes care of the preventive side of dental care: cleaning, x-rays, screening and protecting teeth. She sees adults and children.

Cleanings

Rytel checks your gums before she starts, and the cleaning depends on what she finds.

  1. 1

    Routine cleaning

    Plaque and tartar come off your teeth and along the gumline, then Rytel polishes. This is the regular checkup cleaning for healthy gums.

  2. 2

    Gingivitis cleaning

    For gums that are red, swollen or bleed when you brush. More time goes into the gums, to bring the inflammation down.

  3. 3

    Periodontal cleaning

    For gum disease that has gone below the gumline. Rytel cleans deeper, around the roots of the teeth.

Checks and protection

  1. 4

    Oral cancer screening

    Rytel looks over your lips, tongue, cheeks and throat for sores or changes that need a closer look.

  2. 5

    X-rays

    Radiographs show decay between teeth and bone loss that you can't see in the mirror. Rytel takes them at the studio.

  3. 6

    Fluoride

    A fluoride treatment strengthens enamel. Common for kids, and useful for adults with sensitive teeth or a history of cavities.

  4. 7

    Sealants

    A thin coating painted onto the chewing surfaces of back teeth. Kids get them most often, on new molars.

  5. 8

    Whitening

    In-office whitening with GLO Science. See the whitening page

Children

Rytel sees children as well as adults, and MaineCare covers both. Parents can book and move appointments by text.

Foster families use the studio too. One foster parent wrote that Rytel "makes it easy to get kids seen promptly and does an excellent job coordinating referrals to help them get caught up and stay on track with their dental health."

When you need a dentist

Fillings, crowns, extractions and other treatment come from a dentist. If your cleaning or x-rays turn up a problem, Rytel tells you what she found and refers you to a dentist for the next step.

You can keep seeing Rytel for cleanings while a dentist handles the treatment.
