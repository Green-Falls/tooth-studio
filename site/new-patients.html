New patients

The studio is taking new patients, adults and children. Booking happens by text or phone, and most visits are covered by Delta Dental or MaineCare.

Booking

Text or call (207) 599-6459, or send a visit request. Messages on Facebook or Instagram and email to toothstudiomaine@gmail.com work too.

Bring your insurance card to your first visit.

Hours

There are no fixed posted hours. Say which days suit you, and you'll get open times back. Reviewers mention flexible scheduling, and some have had Sunday appointments.

Questions

Is Rytel a dentist?

No. She's an Independent Practice Dental Hygienist, licensed in Maine to clean teeth, take x-rays and give preventive care in her own practice. For fillings and other treatment she refers you to a dentist.

Can I come here and keep my dentist?

Yes. You can have your cleanings at Tooth Studio and see a dentist for treatment.

Do you see children?

Yes, kids and adults. MaineCare covers both.

Is the building accessible?

The entrance at 55 Main Street has a ramp, the studio is on the first floor, and accessible parking is behind the building.

Can I text instead of calling?

Yes. Text (207) 599-6459 to book or change an appointment.

Can a cleaning happen at a care home?

Residents of Woodlands Memory Care of Bridgton already get on-site visits. For another facility, text or email to ask. Visits outside the studio
