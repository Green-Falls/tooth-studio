New patients

The studio is taking new patients, adults and children. Here's how booking and payment work.

Booking

Text or call (207) 599-6459. You can also send a message on Facebook or Instagram, or email toothstudiomaine@gmail.com.

The studio doesn't post fixed hours. Tell Rytel when you're free and she'll offer a time. Patients mention her flexible hours in reviews, and some have booked Sunday visits.

Bring your insurance card to your first visit.

Insurance

  • Delta Dental

    In network with all Delta Dental plans, including Martin's Point plans that go through Delta Dental.

  • MaineCare

    A MaineCare provider for adults and children.

  • Medicare

    It depends on the plan. Martin's Point is accepted. Message the studio with your plan name and Rytel will check.

  • Other insurance

    Message with your plan name before you book.

No insurance

You can pay for each visit yourself. The studio also has a membership plan for patients without insurance. Ask Rytel what it includes and what it costs.

Using insurance? Most dental plans reset on January 1. If you have a cleaning left this year, book it before December.

Questions

Is Rytel a dentist?
No. She's an Independent Practice Dental Hygienist, licensed to clean teeth, take x-rays and do preventive care in her own practice. For fillings and other treatment she refers you to a dentist.
Can I come here and still keep my dentist?
Yes. Lots of people see a hygienist for cleanings and a dentist for treatment.
Do you see children?
Yes. Rytel sees kids and adults, and MaineCare covers both.
Is the building accessible?
The entrance at 55 Main Street has a ramp, and the studio is on the first floor.
Can I text instead of calling?
Yes. Text (207) 599-6459 to book or change an appointment.
