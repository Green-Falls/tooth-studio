Rytel is an independent practice dental hygienist. She runs the studio herself, cleans your teeth, takes x-rays and checks for gum disease and oral cancer. When you need a filling or other treatment, she refers you to a dentist.

She has worked in dental care for more than ten years. About Rytel and the studio
