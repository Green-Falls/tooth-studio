New patients

The studio is taking new patients, adults and children. Here's how booking and payment work.

Booking

Text or call (207) 599-6459, or use the booking page to write the text for you. You can also message the studio on Facebook or Instagram, or email toothstudiomaine@gmail.com.

Hours

The studio doesn't post fixed hours. Tell Rytel which days suit you and she'll reply with open times. Reviewers mention her flexible scheduling, and some have had Sunday appointments.

Bring your insurance card to your first visit.

Questions

Is Rytel a dentist?

No. She's an Independent Practice Dental Hygienist, licensed to clean teeth, take x-rays and give preventive care in her own practice. For fillings and other treatment she refers you to a dentist.

Can I come here and keep my dentist?

Yes. You can have your cleanings at Tooth Studio and see a dentist for treatment.

Do you see children?

Yes. Rytel sees kids and adults, and MaineCare covers both.

Is the building accessible?

The entrance at 55 Main Street has a ramp, and the studio is on the first floor.

Can I text instead of calling?

Yes. Text (207) 599-6459 to book or change an appointment.

Can Rytel see someone in a care home?

She already visits Woodlands Memory Care of Bridgton. For another facility, text or email to ask. Care outside the studio
