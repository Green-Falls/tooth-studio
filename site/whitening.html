Professional whitening

Since August 2025 Rytel has offered in-office whitening with the GLO Science Professional system. The before-and-after results she has posted each took one appointment.

How it works

  1. Rytel checks your teeth and gums first. Whitening works best on clean, healthy teeth, so she may suggest a cleaning beforehand.
  2. She matches your current color against a shade guide, so you can see the change at the end.
  3. She protects your gums, applies the whitening gel and fits the GLO mouthpiece, which uses light and gentle heat to activate it. The gel goes on in short rounds.
  4. She checks the new shade with you before you leave.

Whitening changes the color of natural enamel. It won't change crowns, veneers or fillings, so ask Rytel how those would match before you book.

Cost

Dental insurance treats whitening as cosmetic and usually doesn't cover it. Text the studio for the current price. Rytel runs specials from time to time, such as $50 off for back-to-school in September 2025, and posts them on Facebook and Instagram.
